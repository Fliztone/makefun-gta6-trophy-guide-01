LEONIDALEGEND

Grand Theft Auto VI · PlayStation 5 · trophy guide & tracker

    About this guide

    Leonida Legend is an offline guide and tracker for all 55 PlayStation 5 trophies of Grand Theft Auto VI (1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze — 28 hidden on PSN until earned). It is a fan-made work written for the make.fun gta6-trophy-guide-01 bounty; it is not affiliated with Rockstar Games and uses no official logos, artwork or game screenshots. Every guide is written in our own words and cites the launch guides and trackers it draws on.

    How to use the tracker

    • Tick a trophy when it pops. Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage) — no account, no network.
    • Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; Import restores it on any machine. Reset clears all marks.
    • Spoiler-safe hides the names, descriptions and guides of unearned story trophies until you mark them earned — the roadmap masks them too.
    • Filter by kind (story, missable, collectibles, activities, online), grade, or free-text search; hide earned trophies to see only what's left.

    Data & honesty policy

    • Official names, descriptions and grades are shown exactly as PlayStation publishes them, per the launch list tracked by PSNProfiles and Exophase (both agree on every entry).
    • The roadmap and missable flags are built from the official descriptions plus the cited launch guides. One trophy is potentially missable — Positive Mental Attitude (Chapter 1 meditation). Nothing else is missable: every questline and collectible survives the story, and nothing the 100% checklist requires can be locked out.
    • Three hidden trophies had no confirmed unlock method at launch (Personal Best, Bad Luck I Guess, Ten Pin). They are marked unverified and this guide does not invent steps for them.
    • Hidden status: 26 of the 28 hidden trophies are identified from PushSquare / PC Gamer spoiler gates; the remaining few gameplay trophies are unconfirmed and shown without a Hidden badge.
    • Tracked counts (250 stunts, 42 range golds, 14 drone locations, 18 meditations, 100 WAiNK scans, 500 criminal-profile actions, …) come from the launch list data and the cited guides.
    • This app makes no network requests: every file it loads ships with it. Source links open only when you follow them.
